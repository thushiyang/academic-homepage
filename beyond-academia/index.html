Coming Home

Travel Far. Return with Clarity.

City gates, cathedral bells, and the light of morning are only the story’s outer shell. Homecoming begins after difficulty, when we choose to return to those we love and understand companionship, responsibility, and everyday life anew.

“Returning” does not end the adventure. It brings the courage gained along the road back into the world we cherish.

Beyond Research

Know the Researcher —and the World the Researcher Cherishes

An academic homepage presents research questions, publications, and projects. Of Love and Family preserves the quieter foundations beneath that work: the support of family, the perspective offered by friends, and the ways life experience shapes judgment and responsibility.

This space brings together two parts of my life: a real-world chronicle of family, friendship, and journeys, and the remarkable opportunities and challenges encountered while traveling through Azeroth.

With Gratitude

  • To family, for giving every journey a place to return to
  • To friends, for walking beside me through uncertain roads
  • To mentors and colleagues, for trust, patience, and shared purpose
  • To the worlds—real and imagined—that continue to inspire curiosity
