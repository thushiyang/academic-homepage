Next-Generation Module Updating

Pathways for Resilient Planning of Atmospheric and Quasi-terrestrial Water Allocation (PREP-AQUA)

PREP-AQUA is being developed as a modular decision-support framework for the coordinated allocation of atmospheric and terrestrial water resources. It integrates precipitation enhancement, ecological water replenishment, groundwater recovery, flood-risk constraints, and physics-guided digital-twin optimization to support resilient, adaptive, and evidence-based water-resource planning.
