Pathways for Resilient Planning of Atmospheric and Quasi-terrestrial Water Allocation (PREP-AQUA)

PREP-AQUA is being designed as a modular interface for joint allocation of atmospheric water resources and terrestrial water resources. The preview concept connects precipitation enhancement, ecological replenishment, groundwater recovery, flood-risk constraints, and digital-twin dispatching into one expandable software pathway.
